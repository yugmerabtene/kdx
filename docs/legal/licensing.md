# Licensing Model

KodPix uses a dual-license model to preserve author rights and support commercial adoption.

## Open-Source Path

- License: `GPL-3.0-only`
- File: `LICENSE`
- Suitable for projects that can comply with GPL obligations.

## Commercial Path

- License: separate commercial agreement
- File: `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`
- Suitable for proprietary or closed-source commercial distribution.

## Attribution and Paternity

- Author attribution remains required in legal notices and distributions.
- Project author identity: `yugmerabtene`.
- Commercial contact: `yug.merabtene@gmail.com`.

## Trademarks

Open-source and commercial license paths do not automatically grant trademark endorsement rights.
